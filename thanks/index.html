Your payment went through. Here is how to get set up.

The key is saved in your Mac’s Keychain, so updates don’t ask for it again. Keep the key somewhere safe: you need it if you move MacEx to another Mac. Questions? Email support.

  1. Download MacEx

    Open the disk image and drag MacEx into your Applications folder. It needs macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

    Download MacEx for Mac

  2. Open it

    If macOS blocks the first launch, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway for MacEx.

  3. Activate with your license key

    MacEx asks for a license key the first time you open it. Paste the key below into the activation window and press Activate. You need an internet connection for this one step.

    Your license key is in the receipt email from Dodo Payments, sent to the address you paid with. If you can’t find it, check spam or email support.
